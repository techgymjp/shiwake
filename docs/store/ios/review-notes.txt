■ メモ（ここから下をそのまま貼る）
====================================================================

1. Purpose and audience: Fish Shiwake is a free quiz game for ages 4+. A sushi chef asks for a fish, and the player picks the right one from two look-alike fish (for example, olive flounder vs. righteye flounder). After each answer, a short tip explains how to tell them apart. There are 100 pairs, and a Fish Guide lists all of them.

2. How to use: No login or account is needed. Choose a category on the title screen and tap "板場に立つ！" (Start). Tap the fish that the chef asked for. The game supports English and Japanese: tap "English" at the top right to switch the language.

3. External services: Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) with Google's consent form (UMP) and App Tracking Transparency. Rewarded ads are optional: after a wrong answer, watching one lets the player retry with a different question (up to 3 times per game). No other services or in-app purchases.

4. Regional differences: None, except that the ad consent form appears only where required (e.g. EEA/UK).

5. Regulated content / third-party material: The game is not in a regulated industry. All illustrations, sounds and text are original.

6. Originality (Guideline 4.3): Fish Shiwake is an original app that we designed and built ourselves. It does not copy or imitate any other app, and it is not made from a template, a purchased source code or a re-skin of another game. The quiz format (a sushi chef orders a fish and the player picks it from two look-alikes) and all of its content were created specifically for this app: 145 fish illustrations, each drawn to show the feature that tells it apart from its look-alike; 100 hand-written "how to tell them apart" tips in English and Japanese; and a sushi chef host character with his own lines and reactions.

7. App functionality (Guideline 4.2): The app is not a website wrapper. All game content (code, illustrations, sounds and texts for 100 pairs) is bundled in the app and fully playable offline. It also uses native iOS features such as the share sheet and App Tracking Transparency.

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Fish Shiwake. We would like to explain why Fish Shiwake is an original app with its own content, and not a copy, a template or a re-skin of another app.

1. Original concept
Fish Shiwake is a fish identification quiz. A sushi chef orders a fish, and the player picks it from two look-alike fish, such as olive flounder vs. righteye flounder, conger eel vs. freshwater eel, or bluefin tuna vs. skipjack tuna. We designed this format ourselves, and the app was built from scratch for it.

2. Content created only for this app
- 145 fish and sea creature illustrations. Each one is drawn to show the feature that tells it apart from its look-alike (spots, stripes, fin shapes, jaw shapes, barbels and so on).
- 100 hand-written "how to tell them apart" tips, in English and Japanese.
- A sushi chef host character with his own lines and reactions to correct and wrong answers.
- Music and sound effects made for this app.

3. Value for players
The app teaches practical knowledge that people use at fish markets, at sushi restaurants and when fishing. Questions go from easy to hard, a Fish Guide lists all 100 pairs with their tips, and players can review the fish they got wrong after each game. The app works fully offline and supports English and Japanese.

4. A single app
Fish Shiwake is a single app with its own Bundle ID. We have not submitted other versions or variations of this app.

We hope this explains that Fish Shiwake offers its own experience. Please let us know if you need any more information.

Best regards,
