[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
A sushi chef asks you for a fish. Two fish that look almost the same appear on the screen. Choose the fish that the chef asked for.
For example: olive flounder or righteye flounder? Conger eel or freshwater eel? Bluefin tuna or skipjack tuna?
Look carefully at the pictures and find the differences.

■ 100 PAIRS OF FISH
• There are 145 kinds of fish and sea animals in 100 pairs.
• There are four categories: sushi and market fish, fish of the coast, fish of rivers and ponds, and squid, shrimp and shellfish.
• You can choose one category or play with all of them.
• One game has 10 questions. The questions start easy and become harder.

■ LEARN HOW TO TELL THEM APART
• After each answer, the game shows a short tip. The tip explains the difference between the two fish.
• For example, the red sea bream has a black edge on its tail fin.
• You can read all 100 tips in the Fish Guide at any time.

■ PLAY WITH THE SUSHI CHEF
• The chef gives you orders. He is happy when you are right. He makes a joke when you are wrong.
• If you answer correctly many times in a row, you get a combo.
• You get a title at the end of the game. The best title is "Master Fish-Spotter".

■ RANKING AND SHARING
• The game saves a ranking for each category on your device. The ranking uses the number of correct answers and the time.
• You can share your result with your friends.
• If you make a mistake, you can watch an ad. Then you get a different question instead. You can do this up to 3 times in one game.

■ OTHER FEATURES
• Colorful design, sound effects and music
• You can play without an internet connection. Ads and sharing need a connection.
• English and Japanese
