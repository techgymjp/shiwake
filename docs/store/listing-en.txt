"Hey! One olive flounder, please!"
Your sushi chef shouts an order, and two look-alike fish appear. Which one is right?
Olive flounder or righteye flounder? Conger eel or freshwater eel? Bluefin tuna or skipjack?
Compare the hand-drawn illustrations and prove your fish-spotting eye.

■ 100 PAIRS, 145 KINDS OF LOOK-ALIKES
• Four categories: Sushi & Market, Rocky Shore & Coast, Rivers & Ponds, and Squid, Shrimp & Shellfish
• Pick a category, or mix them all
• 10 questions per round, going from Easy to Hard

■ LEARN "HOW TO TELL" EVERY TIME
• After each answer, a short tip explains how to tell the two apart
• "Hirame faces left, karei faces right", "The red sea bream has a black-edged tail"... handy at the fish market or on a fishing trip
• Browse all 100 tips anytime in the Fish Guide

■ BEHIND THE COUNTER WITH THE SUSHI CHEF
• The chef places the orders, cheers when you're right, and teases you when you're wrong
• Answer in a row to build a combo
• Earn titles from "Dishwasher Trainee" up to "Master Fish-Spotter"

■ RANKING AND SHARING
• Per-category ranking by correct answers and time (saved on this device)
• Share your result with a row of ⭕ and ❌
• Missed one? Watch an ad to retry with a different question (up to 3 times per round)

■ AND MORE
• Bright, poppy design with sound effects and music
• Play offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
